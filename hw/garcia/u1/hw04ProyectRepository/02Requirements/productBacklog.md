# **Product Backlog** 

# **Requirements** 

# **Functional requirements** 

PB-01: The system must allow the owner to log in using an username and password to keep their data safe at all times 

PB-02: The system shall allow the owner to add new products to the menu. 

PB-03: The system shall calculate the estimated production cost of a product based on its ingredients and their costs. PB-04:The system shall calculate the profit margin of each product based on its production cost and selling price. 

PB-05:The system shall record transactions made through electronic banking. 

PB-06:The system shall record transactions made in cash. 

PB-07:The system shall maintain a chronological record of the business's income and 

expenses. PB-08:The system shall allow the owner to register business expenses, including purchases of raw materials and other supplies. 

PB-09:The system shall automatically calculate the total cost of an order based on the selected products and quantities based on a digital menu interface. 

PB-10:The system shall automatically add the corresponding packaging cost when an order is marked as takeaway. 

PB-11:The system shall automatically deduct the ingredients and supplies used in an order from the available inventory based on the ingredients associated with each product. PB-12:The system shall notify the owner when an ingredient or inventory item reaches its minimum stock level. 

PB-13:The system shall allow the owner to define the ingredients and quantities required to prepare each product. PB-14:The system shall allow the owner to increase inventory quantities when new supplies are purchased. 

PB-15:The system shall prevent or indicate the sale of products whose required ingredients are unavailable or insufficient. 

# **Non - Functional requirements** 

NFR-01:The system shall provide an intuitive and easy-to-use interface that allows employees to create orders with minimal training. 

NFR-02:The system shall calculate order totals and update inventory within a maximum of 2 seconds after an order is submitted. 

NFR-03:The system shall prevent the loss of transaction, inventory, and product information in the event of an unexpected system failure. 

NFR-04:The system shall maintain records of relevant changes to products, inventory, expenses, and transactions for later review. 

NFR-05:Notifications and inventory updates shall be generated promptly after a transaction is completed.
